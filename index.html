Frequently Asked Questions
Our tool uses the underlying game engine mathematics (yaw values) to find a perfect 1:1 physical ratio. It converts your current sensitivity into a universal baseline, then applies the specific engine multiplier of your target game to ensure your muscle memory remains perfectly intact.
The sensitivity slider in the GTA 5 menu is non-linear and unreliable for perfect muscle memory. Our tool calculates your true raw sensitivity based on the game's configuration files (settings.xml), matching the standard 1:1 ratio used by Source Engine games.
cm/360 refers to the physical distance in centimeters you need to move your mouse across your mousepad to perform a full 360-degree rotation in-game. It is the most accurate way to match aiming feel across different game engines.
